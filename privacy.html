Home / Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Information you provide

If you contact MISK BEAUTY LOUNGE or submit an appointment request, you may provide details such as your name, phone number, preferred service, date and message. This static website does not send form data to a website database. Booking and contact forms open WhatsApp with the information you entered so you can review and send it yourself.

2. How information is used

Information you choose to send may be used to respond to your enquiry, discuss services and coordinate appointment requests. Please do not send sensitive personal or payment information through these forms.

3. Cookies and local storage

This website uses browser local storage to remember whether you dismissed or accepted the cookie notice. The website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies in this starter version.

4. Third-party services

Images and video may be loaded from third-party media providers. WhatsApp, social media and other external links are governed by their own privacy policies. External services may collect technical information when you visit them.

5. Data security and retention

Because this is a static website, it does not maintain a customer database. Messages you send through WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp and the recipient account. Avoid sharing information you do not want transmitted.

6. Your choices

You can choose not to submit a form, contact us by phone, or manage local browser data through your browser settings.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact MISK BEAUTY LOUNGE at 03259567575.
